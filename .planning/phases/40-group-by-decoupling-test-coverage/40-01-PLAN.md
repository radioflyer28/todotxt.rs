<objective>
Introduce the `GroupByCategory` type and decouple group-by dimension from sort order in pane state.

Purpose: GRP-01 and GRP-04 require each pane to have an independent group-by dimension. Currently `group_key_for()` takes `&SortOrder` which conflates sorting and grouping — after this plan they are separate controls.

Output:
- `GroupByCategory` enum in `config.rs` (serializable, 4 variants)
- `Pane.group_by: GroupByCategory` field in `state.rs`
- `PaneConfig.group_by: Option<GroupByCategory>` field in `config.rs`
- `group_key_for()` refactored to `&GroupByCategory`
- All `rebuild_*` methods updated to use `pane.group_by` for header keys
- `panes_from_config()` maps `pane_cfg.group_by` → `pane.group_by`
</objective>
