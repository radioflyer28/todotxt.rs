requirement checklist still leaves Phases 46-49 unchecked.

- Recurrence requirements (`REC-01` through `REC-04`) remain unchecked
- Rotation requirements (`DONE-01` through `DONE-03`) remain unchecked
- Filter requirements (`FILT-01` through `FILT-03`) remain unchecked

**Impact:** Milestone audit cannot claim a clean multi-source completion picture yet.

The roadmap milestone banner still shows v1.6.3 as in progress even though the phase roster
and `STATE.md` now reflect full execution and validation completion.

**Impact:** Closeout inconsistency. This could mislead the next milestone or archive workflow.
