## Overview

v1.3 delivered parity-focused TUI workflows for todotxt.net migrants: robust multi-selection,
bulk delete/append flows, shared normalization logic in todotxt-core, configurable keymaps,
and in-app discoverability improvements (help overlay and error log surface).
